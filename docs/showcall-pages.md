# Prompt/Showcall pages, dividers, and headers

## Prompt/Showcall pages

Enable the feature under **Options → General**. A blank page is paired with every source PDF page and can be placed consistently on the left or right.

The source page and Prompt/Showcall page form one editing surface, so cue lines and margins can cross the boundary. Switching sides preserves equivalent content positions. A Prompt/Showcall page cannot be disabled while cues or dividers occupy it.

During export, each blank page becomes a separate PDF page next to its corresponding source page in sequence.

## Dividers

Dividers are shared vertical guides. A divider created on a PDF surface appears on every PDF page; one created on the calling surface appears on every Prompt/Showcall page. Dividers share colour and line-width settings.

## Headers

Headers are shared text items with common colour and text-size settings. Rename them in Options or by double-clicking on the canvas. Headers can move between PDF and calling surfaces.

## Locking and reset

**Lock dividers and headers** prevents moving, editing, and deleting them. **Reset dividers and headers** removes all shared items after confirmation and can be restored through Undo while it remains in history.
